Gift Card Zero-Waste Checkout

What the problem is

Given a prepaid gift card with a fixed balance B and a list of items with prices (each purchasable at most once), select a subset whose total price is as close to B as possible without exceeding it.

Why it matters in real life

Millions of prepaid cards and corporate meal or wellness vouchers go partly unspent every year. Small leftovers can’t buy a standalone item and split payments are often restricted, so a smarter checkout recovers that money.

What this program computes

For your balance and prices, each of the four algorithms shows the items it selects, the total spent and the remaining balance.
